# Aviation Exchange — advisory Jerry research

The marketplace is branded Aviation Exchange. Existing /aircraft-for-sale URLs and compatibility redirects remain valid.

The owner selected recommendation-only research: verified accounts remain usable, and administrators make listing approval decisions. New verified business-account profiles and submitted listing revisions queue durable research. Account name, email-domain or business-website changes queue a new account report. Staff can request research again from the protected account or review panel. No AI output grants roles, blocks accounts, deletes data, approves or publishes listings.

Jerry’s scoped research component uses the Workers AI binding with @cf/openai/gpt-oss-120b for a structured advisory summary. It has no execution tools or administrative credentials. The main customer-chat service and its model policy are unchanged. Deterministic collectors retrieve public MX records, an optional business website (or a non-personal email domain’s public homepage), and official FAA N-number results for aircraft. They do not send verification emails or claim to establish a mailbox’s existence. Website destinations and every redirect require HTTPS, public DNS and public addresses; the Worker retains globally-public-fetch isolation. Responses are size/time bounded, and website text is inert quoted data. Contact telephone, precise location, mailbox local-part and sensitive specification keys are excluded from model input.

FAA comparisons retain manufacturer, model, year, serial number, registration status, field comparisons, source links and collection time. Reserved/multiple/no-record numbers, differences, unavailable pages and malformed AI responses require attention; none is labeled proof of fraud. Owner names and addresses from the registry are not retained. Neither registry matching, MX records nor a reachable website proves seller authority, ownership, business legitimacy or airworthiness. The public marketplace’s existing no-vetting/no-pre-buy scope remains accurate.

Reports include sources, timestamps and clear uncertainty states in the administrator workspace. Jobs are leased durably, run inference outside the business-request queue, discard stale results, cap retries, and limit repeat research per record. A global lease prevents concurrent research overload. No new database/schema migration is required; reports are part of the existing private profile/listing records and disappear with their parent record. Private reports are stripped from public listing responses.

Coverage includes hostile website text, private-network/redirect rejection, FAA mismatch/unavailability, malformed model output, leases, restarts, stale revisions, verified-account usability, pending-listing invariance, and rendered administrator evidence panels. Live qualification uses public/synthetic evidence without creating or moderating production accounts or listings.
