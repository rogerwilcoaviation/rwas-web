Self-hosted for editorial headings, with Arial used for reading and application controls.

The `source-serif-4-latin-*` files are subsets of these originals made with FontTools 4.60.2 for common English, Portuguese, and Spanish glyphs, punctuation, and symbols. Both weight and optical-size variation axes are retained. The shared typography stylesheet selects them with `unicode-range`; the complete upstream files remain available for all other glyphs. The license above also covers the subsets.
